Why serverless

No servers to patch, scale, or pay for at idle — Lambda and API Gateway both bill per request with a generous free tier, and CloudFront caches static assets at the edge so most visits never touch the backend. CloudWatch billing alerts catch runaway usage early, and conversation logs get pruned periodically to keep S3 storage costs flat. Designed to stay under ~$5/month at moderate traffic.

Why this matters

Engineers routinely remove directly biased features (race, gender) but miss indirect proxies — zip code correlating with race, tenure correlating with age. The EEOC Four-Fifths Rule provides a legal threshold most ML pipelines are never tested against. This agent makes compliance auditing automatic: it surfaces violations, traces them to the proxies causing them, and tells the data team exactly what to fix.

Key findings

Top attrition predictors: OverTime status, Monthly Income, Job Satisfaction score, Years Since Last Promotion, and Distance From Home. Logistic Regression was recommended over the Decision Tree for its probabilistic output — giving HR a ranked list of at-risk employees rather than a binary flag, enabling tiered retention interventions rather than blanket programs.

Key insight

Remote work adoption index emerged as the strongest novel predictor — capturing the geographic demand shift from dense metros to secondary markets.
